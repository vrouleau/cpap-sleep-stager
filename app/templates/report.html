How this was measured. CPAP usage, breathing rate and apnea/hypopnea events come from the ResMed machine's own recordings (flow at 25 Hz and its event file); AHI counts only events while asleep. SpO₂, pulse and movement come from a Wellue O2Ring finger oximeter. Sleep stages (awake, light NREM, deep, REM) and arousals are estimated from breathing, pulse and movement by Sleep Stager, open-source software (github.com/vrouleau/cpap-sleep-stager). They are not EEG-based polysomnography, and this is not a medical device.
